# ❝ Who are we, Long-Toolkit?

**Long-Toolkit** was founded by **[Dr Alex Kwong](https://edwebprofiles.ed.ac.uk/profile/dr-alex-kwong)** (Edinburgh) and **[Simon Provost](https://simonprovost.xyz/)** (McGill) during Simon's research internship with the **Lifecourse Epidemiology and Psychiatry Research Group (LEAP)** at the **University of Edinburgh**. Since then, PhD students **[Bianca Branco](https://github.com/b-branco)** (Edinburgh), **[Honghui Chen](https://github.com/ChorasChan)** (Edinburgh) and **[Isaac Tassella](https://github.com/tassellaisaac)** (Edinburgh) have joined discussions, development or the toolkit's use at the University of Edinburgh, all supervised by **[Dr Alex Kwong](https://edwebprofiles.ed.ac.uk/profile/dr-alex-kwong)** (Edinburgh).

Our **open-source Python toolkits** help researchers prepare longitudinal data (repeated measurements of the same participants), compare machine learning with statistical baselines, and explain predictions. A **no-code command-line interface (CLI)** provides access to these tools without writing Python.

Come give it a try: explore the [documentation](https://long-toolkit.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) and the [main repository](https://github.com/Long-Toolkit/long-toolkit). Supports `Python 3.10` only. Available on `macOS` and `Linux`, and on `Windows` (via Docker).
